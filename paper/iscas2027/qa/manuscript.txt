C. Core Interface Tradeoff
   The retained optimized-core comparison records a reduc-
tion from 39 parallel-interface signals to four serial-interface
signals. This is an 89.7% reduction in top-level signal count,
excluding power pads and unused ring resources. It comes with
an increase from 5,082 to 5,296 synthesized cells and from
116,523 to 123,634 µm2 of mapped area, approximately 4.2%
and 6.1%, respectively. The bridge trades serialization latency
and additional logic for a smaller external signal interface.      Fig. 2. KLayout screenshot of the generated NanoCGRA chip GDSII. The
